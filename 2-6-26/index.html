Axelar is a programmable interoperability platform architected to scale Web3 for mass adoption. Unlike other cross-chain connectors, Axelar can run smart-contract logic: it can do anything a blockchain can do including automations that reduce developer overhead and support one-click user experience.

The Axelar platform can connect thousands of chains and dApps across all blockchain ecosystems today and as they grow.
